Hi, this is **Xiao Lin** (online handle: stJL).

This blogging project was shelved from my college days until I stepped into society; after eight long years, I finally decided to learn how to build it.

Here, I plan to document my casual thoughts, discuss freedom-centric operating systems, explore easy passive income, and share what I see and experience in daily life.

I hope my writings will encourage you to stay and read for a while. Feel free to connect with me through comments or email.
